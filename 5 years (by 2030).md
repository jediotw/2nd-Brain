 🏦 Wealth Building Principles

1. **Time is your most valuable asset.**
2. **Money gives you leverage.**
3. **Money solves many problems.**
4. **Money gives you options.**
5. **Money buys back your time.**
6. **Money gives you the freedom to say “No.”**
7. **Money amplifies your value.**
8. **Wealthy people don’t trade time for money.**
9. **Budgeting is good, but increasing income is better than just cutting costs.**
10. **Start investing:**
- Equity
- Mutual funds
- Real estate
- Startups
- Skills
- Personal brand

11. **Have multiple income streams:**
    
    - Active: Job, freelancing
    - Semi-passive: Content, courses
    - Passive: Investments
12. **Understand tax laws and personal finance.**

1. **Build a skill.**
2. **Own equity, assets, and a personal brand—more valuable than a degree.**
3. **Build systems:** Business, automation, content engines.
4. **Use leverage:**
    
    - Code
    - Media
    - People
    - Capital
5. **Modern wealth will be created by:**
    
    - Building products people want
    - Solving problems at scale
    - Playing long-term games with integrity
6. **Be so good at something that you fall in love with it—then sell it to the market.**
7. **Learn to sell, speak, and negotiate.**
8. **Build a side hustle or micro-startup:**
    
    - Freelancing
    - Digital products
    - Micro SaaS/blog/newsletter
    - Paid community

## 💪 Personal Development

1. **Fix your sleep schedule.**
2. **Eat clean:** No junk, high protein, balanced carbs and fats.
3. **Train 5x a week at the gym.**
4. **Walk at least 10,000 steps daily.** 
5. **Improve grooming, style, and posture.**
6. **Surround yourself with high-performance people.**
7. **Cut out negativity, gossip, and draining relationships.**
8. **Delete attention-draining apps.**
9. **Keep your fixed expenses low.**
10. **Build your kingdom first; choose your queen later.**
