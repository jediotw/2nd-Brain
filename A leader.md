**Week 1** – _Clarity First_

- Daily: Shadow 5 min of a leader’s speech (Obama, Indra Nooyi, Jacinda Ardern).
    
- Self-talk: 2 min daily about your day, but with **slow, deliberate, confident tone**.
    
- Vocabulary: 5 “command words” daily (e.g., “vision,” “integrity,” “impact,” “initiative,” “align”). Use each in a sentence aloud.
    

**Week 2** – _Sound Like You Mean It_

- Daily: 5 min shadowing + 5 min _record and listen_ practice.
    
- Practice “power pause” — pause intentionally to emphasize key points.
    
- Reading aloud from leadership articles for clear pronunciation.
    

**Week 3** – _Short Speech Control_

- Write and deliver 1–2 min speeches on leadership topics (record them).
    
- Learn “opening hooks” (questions, quotes, bold statements).
    
- Vocabulary: 5 persuasive phrases daily (“Let’s consider…”, “The key point is…”, “We need to address…”).
    

**Week 4** – _Listening & Thinking Fast_

- Listen to 10 min of TED Talks daily, summarize in 3 sentences.
    
- Impromptu speaking: 1 random question per day, answer without prep for 1 min.
    
- Keep grammar simple but accurate.
    

**Week 5** – _Leading Conversations_

- Practice polite interruptions and steering (“Before we move on…”, “Let’s circle back to…”).
    
- Roleplay with a partner or AI — lead the discussion.
    
- Vocabulary: Meeting leadership phrases.
    

**Week 6** – _Confidence Under Pressure_

- Speak to a camera daily for 3 min, no script.
    
- Debating practice (pick a side and defend it in 1–2 min).
    
- Listen to native debates and copy style.
    

**Week 7** – _Authority in Voice_

- Deep breathing before speaking for calm tone.
    
- Practice lowering pitch slightly for authority.
    
- Storytelling drills: personal story → lesson → call to action.
    

**Week 8** – _Wrap-Up Foundation_

- Deliver a 4-min speech (record, review, improve).
    
- Combine all skills: shadowing + self-talk + impromptu + storytelling.

## **Months 3–4: Persuasion & Impact**

_(Focus: structure, influence, and charisma)_

**Week 9** – _The Leader’s Structure_

- Practice the “3-part speech”: Problem → Solution → Call to Action.
    
- Listen to political or business speeches, note structure.
    

**Week 10** – _Power Vocabulary Expansion_

- Learn 10 leadership collocations weekly (“strategic vision,” “drive results,” “build consensus”).
    
- Use them in short speeches.
    

**Week 11** – _Debate & Influence_

- Join 2–3 online discussions or debates.
    
- Practice disagreeing politely but firmly.
    

**Week 12** – _Persuasive Storytelling_

- Tell 1 short story daily that ends with a motivational lesson.
    
- Watch leadership storytelling videos for inspiration.
    

**Week 13** – _Meetings Like a Boss_

- Simulate a team meeting where you open, guide, and close discussion.
    
- Vocabulary: Meeting openers, transitions, summaries.
    

**Week 14** – _Handling Questions_

- Practice answering “tough” questions without looking nervous.
    
- Roleplay Q&A sessions.
    

**Week 15** – _Fluency Boost Week_

- 20 min/day fast-paced conversation practice (partner or AI).
    
- No pausing for translation — keep talking.
    

**Week 16** – _Midpoint Review_

- Deliver a 5-min leadership speech to a real/virtual audience.
    
- Review recordings from Week 1 to see progress.

## **Months 5–6: Executive Presence & Real-World Mastery**

_(Focus: advanced delivery, spontaneity, and presence)_

**Week 17** – _Polish Pronunciation_

- Target problem sounds, work on clarity without losing naturalness.
    
- Record before/after clips for each session.
    

**Week 18** – _Charisma in Conversation_

- Add humor, warmth, and emotional connection to speech.
    
- Practice active listening and “reflective” replies.
    

**Week 19** – _Negotiation English_

- Learn 10 negotiation phrases (“I understand your concern, however…”, “Let’s find common ground”).
    
- Simulate negotiations.
    

**Week 20** – _High-Stakes Practice_

- Present to a small group or on camera with timer.
    
- Practice recovery techniques if you forget your point.
    

**Week 21** – _Global English Leadership_

- Watch 2–3 leaders from different countries.
    
- Adapt style and vocabulary for diverse audiences.
    

**Week 22** – _Rapid Response Drills_

- Quick-answer Q&A — respond in 5–10 seconds without filler.
    

**Week 23** – _Your Signature Speech_

- Write & refine a 7–10 min keynote on your expertise.
    

**Week 24** – _Final Mastery Week_

- Deliver your keynote to a real audience.
    
- Record, review, and note areas for long-term improvement.





















## **Month 1 – Foundation & Voice Unlock**

**Goal:** Eliminate hesitation, improve pronunciation, and speak 1–2 mins without stopping.

**Week 1:**

- Shadow 5 mins/day of a great speaker (Obama, Steve Jobs).
    
- Record yourself reading a short text aloud.
    
- Practice breathing from your diaphragm (4s inhale, 4s hold, 4s exhale).
    

**Week 2:**

- Shadow 10 mins/day, focusing on sentence rhythm.
    
- Practice tongue twisters for clarity (“She sells seashells…”).
    
- Speak 1 min on a familiar topic without “um/uh”.
    

**Week 3:**

- Shadow 10 mins/day with hand gestures to add energy.
    
- Record & listen for problem sounds (“th”, “r”, “l”).
    
- 2-min storytelling about a personal experience.
    

**Week 4:**

- Shadow 15 mins/day.
    
- Read a short news article, then retell in your own words.
    
- 2–3 min smooth, confident delivery on a chosen topic.
    

---

## **Month 2 – Sentence Flow & Storytelling Basics**

**Goal:** Speak in connected, rhythmic sentences & tell short stories.

**Week 5:**

- Narrate your actions in English 5 mins/day.
    
- Record a 3-min story about your childhood.
    

**Week 6:**

- Practice linking words: and, but, so, because.
    
- Tell a story with 3 parts: hook → build-up → payoff.
    

**Week 7:**

- Watch a short TED Talk, summarize in 3 mins.
    
- Use voice variety (excited, serious, inspiring).
    

**Week 8:**

- Tell a 4-min story with emotional tone changes.
    
- Practice speaking without looking at notes.
    

---

## **Month 3 – Leadership Tone & Body Language**

**Goal:** Sound authoritative & look confident.

**Week 9:**

- Practice power pauses (2s after important points).
    
- Keep a straight posture while speaking.
    

**Week 10:**

- Speak with a downward tone at sentence ends.
    
- Video a 3-min “leader update” speech.
    

**Week 11:**

- Practice eye contact simulation (look at camera lens).
    
- Combine gestures + voice variety.
    

**Week 12:**

- Deliver a 5-min talk standing up.
    
- Use a strong opening line & clear closing statement.
    

---

## **Month 4 – Hosting Skills & Audience Connection**

**Goal:** Control the room, connect with people.

**Week 13:**

- Learn 3 opening lines for hosting an event.
    
- Practice smiling naturally while speaking.
    

**Week 14:**

- Introduce a “guest” (real or imaginary) in 2 mins.
    
- Practice short transitions between topics.
    

**Week 15:**

- Watch professional event hosts & note their style.
    
- Rehearse a 7-min mock event intro.
    

**Week 16:**

- Run a mock event: open → introduce guest → close.
    
- Practice light humor in delivery.
    

---

## **Month 5 – High-Pressure Speaking & Improvisation**

**Goal:** Handle surprises & think fast.

**Week 17:**

- Do 5-min random topic improvisation daily.
    
- Practice answering unexpected questions in 10s.
    

**Week 18:**

- Simulate hosting with interruptions.
    
- Practice recovering from mistakes smoothly.
    

**Week 19:**

- Combine improvisation + audience connection.
    
- Speak 6–7 mins without script.
    

**Week 20:**

- Record full event intro + transition + closing under pressure.
    

---

## **Month 6 – Event Simulation & Final Polish**

**Goal:** Host like a professional.

**Week 21:**

- Perfect your opening & closing lines.
    
- Practice varying energy over a full speech.
    

**Week 22:**

- Full 10-min event rehearsal with camera.
    
- Review and adjust body language & voice.
    

**Week 23:**

- Rehearse with live audience (friends, colleagues).
    
- Practice handling last-minute changes.
    

**Week 24:**

- Final performance: Record a full simulated event.
    
- Review for final polish before real event.
