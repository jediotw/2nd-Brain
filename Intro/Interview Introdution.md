## 🌱 Present Tenses (Now or Regular Actions)

1. Simple Present:  
      
    

- Formula: Subject + V1 (base verb)
    
- Use: Things that always happen or habits.
    
- Keywords: Always, usually, every day.
    
- Example: I eat lunch at 1 PM.
    

3. Present Continuous:  
      
    

- Formula: is/am/are + V4 (verb + ing)
    
- Use: Action happening right now.
    
- Keywords: Now, at the moment.
    
- Example: I am eating lunch now.
    

5. Present Perfect:  
      
    

- Formula: has/have + V3 (past participle)
    
- Use: Action finished, but it still matters now.
    
- Keywords: Just, already, yet.
    
- Example: I have eaten lunch. (I’m not hungry now.)
    

7. Present Perfect Continuous:  
      
    

- Formula: has/have been + V4
    
- Use: Action started before and is still happening.
    
- Keywords: For, since, lately.
    
- Example: I have been eating for 10 minutes.

## 🔙 Past Tenses (Before Now)

1. Simple Past:  
      
    

- Formula: Subject + V2 (past verb)
    
- Use: Action finished in the past.
    
- Keywords: Yesterday, last week, in 2020.
    
- Example: I ate lunch yesterday.
    

3. Past Continuous:  
      
    

- Formula: was/were + V4
    
- Use: Action was happening at a past time.
    
- Keywords: While, when.
    
- Example: I was eating when you called.
    

5. Past Perfect:  
      
    

- Formula: had + V3
    
- Use: Action finished before another past action.
    
- Keywords: Before, after, by the time.
    
- Example: I had eaten before you arrived.
    

7. Past Perfect Continuous:  
      
    

- Formula: had been + V4
    
- Use: Action was happening for some time before another past event.
    
- Keywords: For, since.
    
- Example: I had been eating for 10 minutes when you came.
    

---

## 🚀 Future Tenses (After Now)

1. Simple Future:  
      
    

- Formula: will + V1
    
- Use: Action will happen later.
    
- Keywords: Tomorrow, next week.
    
- Example: I will eat lunch tomorrow.
    

3. Future Continuous:  
      
    

- Formula: will be + V4
    
- Use: Action will be happening at a future time.
    
- Keywords: At this time tomorrow.
    
- Example: I will be eating lunch at 1 PM tomorrow.
    

5. Future Perfect:  
      
    

- Formula: will have + V3
    
- Use: Action will be finished before another future event.
    
- Keywords: By the time, before.
    
- Example: I will have eaten before you arrive.
    

7. Future Perfect Continuous:  
      
    

- Formula: will have been + V4
    
- Use: Action will continue for some time before a future event.
    
- Keywords: For, since, by the time.
    
- Example: I will have been eating for 10 minutes when you arrive.
