**1. Personal Profile**

*   **Face Shape:** Oval-Rectangular hybrid (longer vertical profile, defined jawline).
*   **Facial Structure:** Forehead, cheekbones, and jawline are relatively aligned in width. Jawline is slightly squarer than the forehead.
*   **Skin Tone:** Medium brown, warm-neutral undertone (potentially golden/peachy). Healthy texture noted.

**5. Grooming**

*   **A. Hairstyle:** Medium length hair with volume at the top is recommended.
    *   **Suggested Styles:** Messy textured quiff, Slight side-swept fringe, Light-medium volume style, Slicked back with slight natural volume, Textured spikes or volume blowout, Slick side part with a slight wave.

*   **B. Beard Style:** A light stubble or defined short beard to add angularity and sharpen the jawline slightly.
    *   **Suggested Styles:** Short boxed beard, Faded beard, Light stubble.

*   **C. Makeup (Minimal & Natural):**
    *   **Routine:** Wash -> Moisturizer -> Sunscreen (if needed) -> BB Cream -> Under Eye Concealer (dots) -> Brush Eyebrows Upward -> Lip Balm.
    *   **BB Cream:** Apply lightly, focusing on the T-Zone.
        *   *Products:* Lab Series BB Tinted Moisturizer, Missha BB Cream No. 23 (use sparingly), Erborian BB Homme.
        *   *Application:* Half pea size, dab on forehead/cheeks/nose/chin, blend lightly with fingers/sponge for even tone (not visible coverage).
    *   **Lip Balm:**
        *   *Shades/Types:* Classic Pink, Rose/Mauve, Nude.
        *   *Products:* Vaseline Rosy Lips, Burt’s Bees Tinted Lip Balm (Hibiscus), Jack Black Intense Therapy Balm (no tint).

**7. Photography Tips**

*   **Lighting:** Use natural light (face a window). Avoid direct overhead lighting.
*   **Camera Angle:** Position at or slightly below eye level.
*   **Posing:** Slight head tilt (10-15°), relax shoulders, relax mouth (small natural smile or neutral/soft expression).
